ABOUT US

We are Los Cantaritos Mexican Grill

At Los Cantaritos Mexican Grille, we are passionate about bringing the authentic flavors of Mexico to Medina, Ohio. We prepare fresh, flavorful Mexican dishes using quality ingredients and traditional flavors that make every meal special. Whether you’re joining us for a casual meal with family and friends or simply craving delicious Mexican food, we’re here to make you feel welcome. Come visit us and enjoy a taste of Mexico!
